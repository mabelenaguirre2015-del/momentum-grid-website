01 THE REAL PROBLEM

Posting more isn't the same as moving forward.

You already have the knowledge. The problem is that your content can attract the wrong audience—or people who never become clients.

When you don't understand why people stop watching, it's easy to keep changing things at random: another hook, another format, another posting schedule.

Before you make more content, understand what your current content is telling you.

05 BEFORE YOU START

Not sure if an audit is the right next step? Start here.

What exactly is included? +

A review of your last 10 short-form posts, focusing on hooks, messaging, structure, audience alignment, and calls to action. You'll receive a prioritized list of recommendations.

Will this guarantee more views or clients? +

No. Content performance depends on many factors, and no audit can honestly guarantee views, leads, or sales. The goal is to identify practical improvements and help you make more informed content decisions.

Who is this designed for? +

Coaches and educators who teach habits, productivity, procrastination management, and self-discipline, and who use short-form content to communicate their expertise.

What happens after I reach out? +

Send a short message about your content and offer. We'll clarify whether the audit fits your needs and confirm the scope and next steps before any work begins.
